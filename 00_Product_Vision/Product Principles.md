# Product Principles
Product principles are the rules that the entire product team promises to follow — the rules we will never compromise on while building our product.

#### 1. Customer Obsessed
Start with the customer's problem and work backwards.

#### 2. Seek the Truth
Follow evidence, challenge assumptions, and make uncertainty visible.

#### 3. See the Whole
Understand every decision in its wider organizational context.

#### 4. Own the Outcome
Measure success by the impact we create, not the information we produce.

#### 5. Make Complexity Clear
Turn organizational complexity into clarity people can understand and act on.

#### 6. Keep Learning
Use evidence, feedback, and outcomes to continuously improve what we build
