## Product DNA - explains why we exist 

> **Connect organizational dependencies.**

### Why this matters

Organizations today operate through multiple departments, systems, and workflows. While every department performs its own function, the relationships and dependencies between them are often invisible. Our product exists to connect these organizational dependencies, creating the foundation for better decisions, clearer ownership, and enterprise-wide visibility.
