# User Journey Map — Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform

> **Map the Plant Head's journey from identifying a critical operational event to making an informed decision and initiating follow-up action.**

---

## 1. Journey Objective

The journey evaluates how effectively the product reduces the effort required to move from:

**Event → Understanding → Investigation → Assessment → Decision → Action**

> **The journey is decision-led, not feature-led.**

---

## 2. Primary Persona

**Plant Head**

### Primary Job

> **When a significant operational event occurs, understand its context and determine the appropriate response.**

### Journey Trigger

**A critical operational event or deviation requiring management attention.**

---

## 3. Current-State Journey

| Dimension | Detect | Understand | Investigate | Assess | Decide | Follow Up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **User Objective** | Identify what requires attention | Establish what happened | Determine why it happened | Understand consequences | Determine appropriate response | Ensure action is owned |
| **User Behaviour** | Reviews available alerts / information | Gathers initial event details | Coordinates with relevant functions | Evaluates impact and recurrence | Weighs available response options | Assigns / reviews next action |
| **Key Question** | What happened? | What exactly occurred? | Why did it happen? Who is involved? | What is affected? Has this happened before? | What should I consider? | Who owns the next step? |
| **Primary Friction** | Relevant information may be distributed across sources | Context requires manual gathering | Cross-functional investigation takes time | Dependencies and downstream impact may be unclear | Decision depends on incomplete or fragmented context | Ownership and follow-up may require coordination |
| **Information Need** | Event, severity, timing | Event details and operating context | Cause, function, ownership, dependencies | Impact, risk, historical occurrences | Consolidated decision context | Decision, owner, action, status |
| **Product Opportunity** | Surface significant events | Consolidate event context | Connect cause, ownership and dependencies | Make impact and recurrence visible | Provide decision-ready context | Capture ownership and next step |
| **Desired Outcome** | Event recognised | Situation understood | Cause and context established | Consequences understood | Informed decision made | Action clearly owned |

---

## 4. Future-State Experience

### 1. Detect

**Product:** Surface significant events requiring attention.

**User Outcome:** The user immediately understands which event requires investigation.

↓

### 2. Understand

**Product:** Consolidate the relevant event information in one view.

**User Outcome:** The user establishes what happened, when and where.

↓

### 3. Investigate

**Product:** Connect cause, ownership and cross-functional dependencies.

**User Outcome:** The user develops a coherent explanation of why the event occurred.

↓

### 4. Assess

**Product:** Present operational impact, dependencies and relevant historical occurrences.

**User Outcome:** The user understands the scale, consequence and recurrence risk.

↓

### 5. Decide

**Product:** Convert the available context into a concise decision-oriented summary.

**User Outcome:** The user can determine the appropriate response with greater confidence.

↓

### 6. Follow Up

**Product:** Capture the decision, accountable owner and next step.

**User Outcome:** The response has clear accountability and can be followed through.

---
