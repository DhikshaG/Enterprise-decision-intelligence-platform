### MVP Definition

> **Define the minimum product experience required to test the decision-context hypothesis.**

---

## 1. MVP Objective

> **Test whether connected enterprise context can help a decision-maker understand a significant business event and determine an informed response.**

---

## 2. Primary User

**Plant Head**

The MVP begins with one decision-maker who requires visibility across multiple plant functions.

---

## 3. Core Decision

### **"What happened, why did it happen, and what should I consider next?"**

---

## 4. Core Experience

**Business Event**
→ **Cause**
→ **Ownership**
→ **Dependencies**
→ **Impact**
→ **History**
→ **Decision Context**

---

## 5. MVP Scope

### IN

- Event-level intelligence
- Cross-functional context
- Root-cause view
- Ownership visibility
- Impact & dependency view
- Recurrence / historical context
- Decision-oriented summary

### OUT

- Enterprise-scale integrations
- Real-time data infrastructure
- Advanced predictive AI
- Automated workflows
- Full process-mining capability
- Multi-site enterprise deployment

---

## 6. MVP Success Test

> **Can the Plant Head reach a sufficiently informed understanding of a significant event without manually assembling context across functions?**

### Evidence to Seek

**Faster understanding**  
**Better context**  
**Clearer decision path**  
**Lower information-assembly effort**

---

## 7. MVP Boundary

> **This is a focused product demonstration, not a production-scale enterprise platform.**

The MVP exists to **test the product hypothesis, demonstrate the decision experience, and establish what should be built next.**
