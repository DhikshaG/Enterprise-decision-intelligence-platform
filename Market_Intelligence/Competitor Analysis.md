### Competitive Analysis

> **Assess the competitive landscape, establish the capability benchmark, and define the appropriate scope for the Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform.**

---

## 1. Executive Assessment

> **Enterprise process intelligence is a mature market. Leading platforms already connect operational data to process performance, root causes, dependencies, impact and recommended actions. The opportunity is therefore not to recreate the category, but to demonstrate a focused decision-intelligence experience.**

---

## 2. Competitive Benchmark

| Capability | Celonis | SAP Signavio | Microsoft Process Mining |
|---|---|---|---|
| Process visibility | Strong | Strong | Strong |
| Root-cause analysis | Strong | Strong | Strong |
| Cross-process dependencies | Strong | Strong | Strong |
| Impact analysis | Strong | Strong | Strong |
| Recommendations | Strong | Strong | Strong |
| Decision support | Strong | Strong | Strong |
| Enterprise scale | Very High | Very High | High |

---

## 3. Competitive Learnings

**Celonis**  
Enterprise-scale process intelligence, combining process data and business context to identify causes, predict outcomes and recommend actions.

**SAP Signavio**  
Process transformation and intelligence, with strong emphasis on cross-process analysis, dependencies and impact.

**Microsoft Process Mining**  
Process discovery and analytics integrated into the Microsoft ecosystem, with process analysis, performance monitoring and root-cause capabilities.

---

## 4. Strategic Implication

### **Do not recreate the category.**

The competitive benchmark establishes that the underlying intelligence capabilities are already mature.

Therefore, the Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform should focus its MVP on:

> **Turning connected enterprise intelligence into decision-ready context.**

---

## 5. Decision Context Lens

**BUSINESS EVENT**  
↓  
**WHAT HAPPENED?**  
↓  
**WHY?**  
↓  
**WHO / WHAT IS INVOLVED?**  
↓  
**WHAT IS THE IMPACT?**  
↓  
**WHAT SHOULD THE DECISION-MAKER CONSIDER?**

---

## 6. MVP Boundary

### In Scope

> **A focused decision-context experience demonstrating how connected enterprise intelligence can support a business decision.**

### Out of Scope

> Enterprise-scale process mining, data infrastructure, advanced AI, automation and full platform capabilities.

---

## 7. Strategic Conclusion

> **The competitive analysis validates the problem space while narrowing the product ambition: the Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform will demonstrate how enterprise intelligence can be translated into decision-ready context, rather than attempt to replicate established process-intelligence platforms.**
