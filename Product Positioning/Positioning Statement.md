### Product Positioning

> **Define where the Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform plays, who it serves, and the value it is designed to create.**

---

## 1. Positioning Framework

**TARGET → JOB → CATEGORY → VALUE → PROOF → COMPETITIVE FRAME**

---

## 2. Positioning

| Dimension | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Target** | Cross-functional decision-makers in complex enterprises |
| **Job** | Make informed decisions when important business events occur |
| **Category** | Enterprise Decision Intelligence |
| **Value** | Connect fragmented enterprise context into decision-ready insight |
| **Proof** | Links events with causes, ownership, dependencies, impact and history |
| **Competitive Frame** | Complements the capabilities established by process-intelligence platforms rather than attempting to replicate them |

---

## 3. Positioning Statement

> **For enterprise decision-makers who need to understand complex business events, the Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform connects cross-functional context into decision-ready insight — helping them understand what happened, why, who is involved, what is affected, and what to consider next.**

---

## 4. Product Lens

### **Decision Context**

**Business Event**  
↓  
**What happened?**  
↓  
**Why?**  
↓  
**Who / What is involved?**  
↓  
**What is the impact?**  
↓  
**What should the decision-maker consider?**

---

## 5. Competitive Boundary

### We are not positioning as:

> **Another process-mining platform.**

### We are positioning as:

> **A focused Enterprise Decision Intelligence experience built around connected business context.**

---

## 6. Strategic Rationale

> **The market validates the underlying intelligence layer. Our product scope is therefore deliberately focused on demonstrating how that intelligence can be organized around the decision-maker and the decision they need to make.**
