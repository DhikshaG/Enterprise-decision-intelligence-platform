### Differentiation Hypothesis

> **Define the strategic product bet to be tested through the MVP.**

---

## Strategic Context

The market already provides mature capabilities across:

**Process Intelligence · Root-Cause Analysis · Dependency Mapping · Impact Analysis · Recommendations**

Therefore, differentiation should not be claimed at the underlying intelligence layer.

---

## Hypothesis

> **The Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform can create distinctive user value by organizing enterprise intelligence around the decision-maker's context — translating complex events into a concise, decision-ready view.**

---

## The Product Bet

### From

**Process / Event → Analysis → Insight**

### To

**Business Event → Context → Understanding → Decision**

---

## Hypothesized Value

The platform brings together:

**WHY** — Cause  
**WHO** — Ownership  
**WHAT ELSE** — Dependencies  
**SO WHAT** — Impact  
**HISTORY** — Recurrence  
**NOW WHAT** — Decision consideration

> **The hypothesis is that this context-first experience can reduce the effort required to understand an important business event and determine the appropriate response.**

---

## What Must Be Proven

| Test | Success Question |
|---|---|
| **Comprehension** | Can users understand the situation faster? |
| **Context** | Can users see the relevant connections without assembling them manually? |
| **Decision Readiness** | Does the experience make the next decision clearer? |
| **Actionability** | Can users identify an appropriate response from the available context? |

---

## Boundary

**Not claimed:** A unique market capability.

**Claimed:** A focused product experience and learning hypothesis.

---

### Status

**Differentiation Hypothesis — To Be Tested**

> The hypothesis will be validated through the MVP and should be revised if evidence does not support it.
