The easiest analogy

Imagine a factory detective:

Microsoft Process Mining:

"Let me reconstruct what happened."

SAP Signavio:

"Let me understand the process and find where it can be improved."

Celonis:

"Let me build a broader operational picture, understand the causes and help you decide and act."

These are emphasis differences, not completely different products.

We are saying:

"I studied how mature platforms solve this category, and I'm going to build a focused mini-version to demonstrate how I, as a Product Analyst, would approach decision-making through a connected context lens."

So our demonstration could take:

Production ↓15%

and present:

WHAT
Production dropped 15%.

WHY
Machine breakdown contributed.

WHO
Mechanical / Engineering involved.

WHAT ELSE
Production and dispatch affected.

HISTORY
Similar breakdown occurred previously.

DECISION CONTEXT
What should the Plant Head investigate or act on?

The underlying intelligence is not our invention.

The product exercise is in how we scope, structure, prioritize, design and demonstrate that intelligence for a decision-maker.
