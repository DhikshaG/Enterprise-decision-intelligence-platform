The easiest analogy

Imagine a factory detective:

Microsoft Process Mining:

"Let me reconstruct what happened."

SAP Signavio:

"Let me understand the process and find where it can be improved."

Celonis:

"Let me build a broader operational picture, understand the causes and help you decide and act."

These are emphasis differences, not completely different products.

We are saying:

"I studied how mature platforms solve this category, and I'm going to build a focused mini-version to demonstrate how I, as a Product Analyst, would approach decision-making through a connected context lens."

So our demonstration could take:

Production ↓15%

and present:

WHAT
Production dropped 15%.

WHY
Machine breakdown contributed.

WHO
Mechanical / Engineering involved.

WHAT ELSE
Production and dispatch affected.

HISTORY
Similar breakdown occurred previously.

DECISION CONTEXT
What should the Plant Head investigate or act on?

The underlying intelligence is not our invention.

The product exercise is in how we scope, structure, prioritize, design and demonstrate that intelligence for a decision-maker.

In simple terms
Platform	I'd describe it as
Celonis	🥇 Strongest specialist / category benchmark
SAP Signavio	🥈 Very strong, especially for SAP/process transformation
Microsoft Process Mining	🥉 Strong, particularly attractive within Microsoft's broader ecosystem

But “most effective” depends on the use case.

Want deep, enterprise-scale process intelligence? → Celonis
Heavily invested in SAP and broader business-process transformation? → Signavio can be very compelling
Already operating heavily in Microsoft/Power Platform? → Microsoft can be attractive

SAP Signavio itself is currently recognized as a leader in Gartner's Process Intelligence Platforms category, and its current capabilities include automated root-cause analysis, value analysis, AI-assisted analysis and operational workflows.

Celonis has also moved beyond traditional process mining into a broader operational intelligence layer for enterprise AI, with customers using it across procurement, supply chain, finance and other processes.

For our Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform

I would therefore use:

Celonis = primary competitive benchmark

and use SAP Signavio + Microsoft Process Mining as secondary benchmarks.

Not because the others are weak. Rather, Celonis gives us the strongest benchmark for understanding what a mature specialist in this category can already accomplish
