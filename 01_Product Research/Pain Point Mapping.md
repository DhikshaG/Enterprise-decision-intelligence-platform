### Pain Point Map

> **Identify the friction that prevents users from accomplishing their critical jobs — and quantify why it matters.**

---

## Executive Synthesis

> **The primary friction is not lack of data; it is the difficulty of connecting fragmented information into actionable cross-functional context.**

This creates four consequential gaps:

**Context → Cause → Accountability → Impact**

---

## Pain Point Assessment

| Priority | Pain Point | Consequence | Business Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Fragmented Context** | Information must be assembled across functions | Slower decisions |
| **2** | **Unclear Cause** | Root cause requires cross-functional investigation | Delayed resolution |
| **3** | **Unclear Accountability** | Ownership is difficult to establish | Slower action |
| **4** | **Limited Impact Visibility** | Downstream effects are not immediately visible | Suboptimal decisions |
| **5** | **Weak Recurrence Learning** | Previous issues may not inform future decisions | Repeated cost / disruption |

---

---

## Highest-Value Opportunity

### **Connect the Context**

> The strongest product opportunity is to reduce the effort required to connect relevant information across organizational boundaries when an important business event occurs.

**Why this matters**

**Fragmented Context**  
→ **Better Understanding**  
→ **Faster Decision**  
→ **Effective Action**  
→ **Reduced Recurrence**

---

## Product Implication

> **Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform should connect the relevant organizational context around a business event and make it actionable for the decision-maker.**
