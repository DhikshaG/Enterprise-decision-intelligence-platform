# Product Personas
---
#### A product persona is a type of persona specifically created to guide product decisions. It is designed around the user's relationship with the product — their jobs, problems, needs, decisions, and desired outcomes — rather than creating a general biography about them
---
For example:

Normal persona

Operations Manager, Priya, 38, Chennai, 12 years experience.

That's just describing a person.

Product persona

We ask:

What does Priya need from our product?

So we document:

Role: Operations Manager

Key responsibility: Make cross-department operational decisions

Typical decision: Why did production fall?

Current problem: Information is scattered across departments

Pain: Takes time to connect the information

Need: Understand cause, dependencies, ownership and impact quickly

Goal: Make faster, informed decisions

Success: Resolve issues and prevent recurrence
