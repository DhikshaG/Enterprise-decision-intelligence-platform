### Jobs To Be Done

> **Define what users need to accomplish — and the outcome they expect.**

---

## JTBD Framework

**SITUATION → JOB → OUTCOME**

> A JTBD describes the user's fundamental goal, not the feature or solution.

---

## Persona JTBD

| Persona | Situation | Core Job | Desired Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plant Head** | Major plant issue occurs | **Make the right plant-level decision** | Resolve the issue and prevent recurrence |
| **Technical Head** | Significant technical issue arises | **Make an informed technical decision** | Resolve the issue with minimal operational impact |
| **Production Head** | Production is disrupted | **Restore production continuity** | Minimize production loss |
| **Quality Head** | Quality deviation occurs | **Identify and address the underlying cause** | Restore quality and prevent recurrence |
| **Engineering Head** | Engineering issue affects operations | **Diagnose and resolve the issue** | Restore reliability and minimize disruption |
| **Mechanical Head** | Mechanical failure occurs | **Restore mechanical reliability** | Minimize downtime and recurrence |
| **Electrical Head** | Electrical failure occurs | **Restore electrical reliability** | Minimize disruption and recurrence |

---

## Core JTBD

> **When an important business event occurs, decision-makers need to understand its organizational context so they can make the right decision and act effectively.**

---

## Product Implication

**Connect → Explain → Enable Decision**

> Enterprise Decision Intelligence Platform should help users move from **what happened** to **why it happened, who is involved, what is affected, and what to do next.**

---

### Evidence Status

**Observation-based working hypothesis**

> JTBDs are derived from professional observation and role analysis; they remain open to refinement as the product evolves.
