### Product Personas

> **Understand who we build for, what they need, and why.**

---

## 1. Persona Assessment Approach

### Objective

Identify the organizational roles most affected by the problem of fragmented cross-functional context and understand how their decisions, dependencies and information needs differ.

### Evidence Approach

**Observation → Pattern → Persona Hypothesis → Product Implication**

Personas are developed using professional observations, publicly available role information and product analysis.

> **Scope:** No confidential company information, internal documents, proprietary workflows or direct user interviews are used.

# 4. Emerging Persona Architecture

The initial assessment suggests three distinct decision layers:

### Plant-Level Decision Makers
**Plant Head · Technical Head**

Require a consolidated view of organizational context to make plant-level decisions.

### Functional Decision Owners
**Production Head · Quality Head · Engineering Head**

Interpret events within their functions while depending on information from other functions.

### Technical Context Owners
**Mechanical Head · Electrical Head**

Generate and manage operational/technical context that contributes to wider decisions.

---

## 5. Initial Product Implication

> **Project Nexus should not be designed as a tool for one department in isolation. Its potential value lies in connecting context generated across functions and making that context usable for cross-functional decision-making.**

---

## 6. Persona Prioritization

Personas will be assessed against five criteria:

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| **Problem Exposure** | How directly does the role experience the identified problem? |
| **Decision Influence** | How much does the role influence consequential decisions? |
| **Cross-Functional Dependency** | How dependent is the role on information from other functions? |
| **Business Impact** | How significant are the outcomes of its decisions? |
| **Product Relevance** | How directly can Project Nexus improve the role's decision-making? |

### Current Working Classification

**Primary Personas**  
Plant Head · Technical Head · Production Head

**Secondary Personas**  
Quality Head · Engineering Head

**Supporting Personas**  
Mechanical Head · Electrical Head

> **Important:** This classification is a working hypothesis based on observation and role analysis. It will remain open to revision as the product is developed.

---

## 7. Key Insight

> **The product opportunity may not be to serve each function independently, but to connect the context generated by functional teams for the people responsible for cross-functional decisions.**
